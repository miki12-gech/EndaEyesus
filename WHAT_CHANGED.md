# What Changed in the Library - User-Friendly Summary

## 🎉 Three Major Improvements Made

### 1. **New Preview Feature** 👀

You can now **view documents right in the app** without opening them in a new tab!

**What to look for**:

- New button on each resource card: 👁 **Preview**
- Click it to see documents in a beautiful pop-up window
- Works with: Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, PDFs, Images, Videos

**How to use**:

1. Click 👁 **Preview** button on any resource
2. Document opens in a full-screen window
3. Can still use the ↓ **Download** button to download the file
4. Click X or outside the window to close preview

---

### 2. **Search Now Works Better** 🔍

Type in the search box to find what you need - results are now **organized by category**!

**What to look for**:

- Search box at the top (same as before)
- But now results are grouped by category
- Only shows categories that have matching results

**How to use**:

1. Type in the search box (e.g., "prayer" or "calculus")
2. Results appear grouped by category
3. Scroll down to see results in each category
4. Can combine search with filters for more specific results

---

### 3. **Categories Are Now Super Clear** 📂

Resources are organized into three **clearly different sections** with **colors and descriptions**.

**The Three Sections**:

#### 📿 **Spiritual Resources** (Purple)

Sacred texts, teachings, and spiritual guidance

- **Color**: Purple headers and backgrounds
- **Icon**: 📿 Prayer beads emoji
- **What's here**: Religious texts, prayers, spiritual teachings

#### 📚 **Academic Resources** (Blue)

Textbooks, exams, courses, and educational materials

- **Color**: Blue headers and backgrounds
- **Icon**: 📚 Books emoji
- **What's here**: Textbooks, past exams, lecture notes, study materials

#### 📁 **Other Resources** (Amber/Gold)

Miscellaneous files and community materials

- **Color**: Amber/gold headers and backgrounds
- **Icon**: 📁 Folder emoji
- **What's here**: Everything else that doesn't fit the other categories

## 🔄 Expand & Collapse Categories

Each category section can be **expanded or collapsed**:

- **Click the category header** to expand/collapse
- The **chevron icon** (▼ or ▲) shows if it's expanded
- **Number badge** shows how many resources are in that category
- Useful if you want to focus on just one type of resource

**Example**: If you only care about academic textbooks, you can collapse the Spiritual and Other sections to reduce scrolling.

## 🔎 Better Filtering

You can still use filters, and they work better now:

**Available Filters**:

1. **Category** - Choose Spiritual, Academic, or Other
2. **Department** - For academic resources (CS, Math, Biology, etc.)
3. **Academic Year** - Filter by year (2024, 2023, etc.)
4. **Document Type** - Textbook or Past Exam

**How to use**:

1. Click **[Show Filters]** button
2. Select what you want to filter by
3. Results update automatically
4. Click **[Hide Filters]** to make more room

### "I want to find a prayer"

1. Scroll to 📿 **Spiritual Resources** section (purple)

3. Click 👁 **Preview** to read the prayer without downloading

### "I need a calculus textbook"

1. Scroll to 📚 **Academic Resources** section (blue)

1. Use filter: Document Type = **Past Exam**

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I still download files like before?**
A: Yes! Click the ⬇️ **Download** button just like before.

**Q: What if preview doesn't work?**
A: Click ⬇️ **Download** to open the file the traditional way, or try the **Try Opening in New Tab** button.

**Q: Can I like resources?**
A: Yes! Click ❤️ **Like** button. The count updates in real-time.

**Q: How do I know which category to look in?**
A: Look for the icon (📿 📚 📁) and the color. Or just search for what you want.

**Q: Do I have to expand all categories?**
A: No! Click the header to collapse categories you don't need. This saves scrolling space.
